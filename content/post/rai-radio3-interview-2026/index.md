On 5 January 2026 I joined __Rai Radio3 Scienza__ — the science show of Italian national public radio — to talk about climate change and Alpine swifts (*Tachymarptis melba*): how heatwaves and extreme weather affect nestlings, what long-term datasets are telling us, and why wildlife rescue centres are unexpectedly powerful allies in tracking these effects.

🎧 Listen to the full episode: [Rai Radio3 Scienza, 05/01/2026](https://www.raiplaysound.it/audio/2026/01/Radio3-Scienza-del-05012026-f66008f7-c688-4466-8e6e-c526084eee3f.html) (in Italian).

If your wildlife rescue centre collects data on swifts, please consider joining the [Saving swifts, understanding climate]({{< relref "/project/rescue-swifts" >}}) project — we are building a Europe-wide collaboration.
