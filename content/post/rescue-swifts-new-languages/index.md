🌍 Big news! The [Europe-wide rescue-centre survey]({{< relref "/project/rescue-swifts" >}}) on swifts and extreme weather is now available in **23 languages** — 18 new ones added to the original 5.

### Why so many languages

Wildlife rescue centres are scattered across the whole continent and often run by volunteers who may not be comfortable filling out a long form in English. To make participation as easy as possible, we have prepared machine translations of the original English form covering most major European languages — from Bulgarian to Ukrainian, passing through Greek, Polish, Portuguese and Turkish.

### How to use them

The native versions (English, Italian, German, Spanish, French) were prepared with native speakers and remain the most accurate. The 18 new versions are **automatic translations**: the meaning is preserved but some wording may sound a little off. If anything is unclear, just write to **giulia.masoero@vogelwarte.ch** and we'll help.

🔗 Pick your language on the project page: [Saving swifts, understanding climate]({{< relref "/project/rescue-swifts" >}}).

### Please help us share

If you know wildlife rescue centres in countries we haven't reached yet — or if you can share the link in your local community — it makes a real difference. The more centres take part, the better we can document the impact of heatwaves and extreme weather on European swift populations.
